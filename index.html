Who I am

About Me

Hi, I'm Raisul. I'm a Lecturer and Program Coordinator in Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University, the same place where I fell in love with computing as a student. I graduated with Highest Distinction (CGPA 3.91) and made the Vice Chancellor's List six times. Before teaching, I built backend systems and APIs for a large enterprise platform. Today I teach programming, algorithms and microprocessors, and helping students reach their own "it finally clicks" moment is one of the best parts of my day.

One question connects all of my research: how do we build computing systems that people can truly trust? To me, that means systems that protect sensitive data, can prove they did the right thing, explain their decisions and keep working correctly when pushed to their limits. I approach this from two sides: designing systems that are trustworthy from the start, and building tools that test them until they break.

On the design side, my work on privacy-preserving federated learning with homomorphic encryption was published in Information Fusion (IF 17.4) and won BRAC University's Quality Journal Publication Award (2025). I also presented explainable deep learning for medical images at IEEE ICCIT 2024. Now I'm adding zero-knowledge proofs so that an untrusted server can prove it combined everyone's data honestly.

On the testing side, I built an agentic, property-based fuzzer that found a bug in the open-source tomlc17 parser, which the maintainer confirmed and fixed upstream. The same habit of questioning assumptions shaped my paper under review at ACM IMWUT. It uncovers hidden methodological errors, including a previously unreported data-loading bug, that explain why activity-recognition models fail across datasets.

Why Cross-Dataset Transfer Fails in Human Activity Recognition: A Confound-Controlled Analysis of Classifier Degeneration

Submitted ACM IMWUT
  • Under review, Proceedings of the ACM on Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies
  • 2026

A confound-controlled evaluation protocol that identifies four methodological errors in prior cross-dataset HAR transfer studies, including a previously unreported PAMAP2 sensor-channel loading error. It introduces a label-free prediction-entropy diagnostic and a per-class CORAL alignment baseline for deployable wearable HAR.

  • Human Activity Recognition
  • Domain Shift

  • CORAL Alignment
  • Evaluation Methodology

Verifiable Federated Aggregation as a Native Data Fabric Primitive (zk-FedMax)

Ongoing Zero-Knowledge Proofs
  • In progress, extends the Information Fusion data fabric work
  • 2026

Adds zero-knowledge proofs to selection-based federated aggregation (FedMax, FedMin, top-k), closing the untrusted-server gap left open in earlier work. The goal is ZK-provable comparison and argmax operations that prove the aggregator is honest without revealing any client update, with convergence guarantees under non-IID data and validation on healthcare imaging datasets.

  • Zero-Knowledge Proofs
  • Federated Learning
  • Verifiable Computation
  • Non-IID Data
